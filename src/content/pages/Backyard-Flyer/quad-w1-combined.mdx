# Quad W1 Combined — W1a vs W1b side-by-side

Two quads fly simultaneously through identical window-gate circuits on parallel tracks 25 m apart. Both experience the same shared wind.

- **Blue (A)** — W1a: carrot-and-stick, direct approach to gate center
- **Orange (B)** — W1b: pre-gate staging, aligns with window normal before entering

```
Track A (z = 0):   gates at x = ±10, z = ±10
Track B (z = 25):  gates at x = ±10, z = 15 / 35
```

The **Loops completed** chart is the key metric: it accumulates every time a drone completes all four gates and returns home. Over many laps the difference in gate miss rate becomes visible as a gap between the two curves.
